Privacy Policy

簡介

KidSpeech（以下稱「本 App」）是一款為 2 至 3 歲幼兒設計的語音學習應用程式，由 CityFun（以下稱「我們」）開發與維運。本政策說明本 App 會處理哪些資料、為什麼要處理、以及資料會傳送到哪裡。

重點摘要：本 App 不需要註冊、不需要帳號，也不會蒐集姓名、生日、電話、電子郵件或任何可以辨識身分的個人資料。

孩子按住錄音鍵時錄下的短音檔，會連同正在練習的詞語一起傳送給 Microsoft Azure AI Speech 評估發音，評分完成後立刻從裝置上刪除，Microsoft 也不會保存。我們不留存、不分析、也不販售任何錄音。

此外，本 App 會從 Google Firebase 下載圖卡內容，訂閱則由 Apple 或 Google 處理。這些都不會讓我們取得任何可以辨識您或孩子身分的資料。

一、我們處理的資料

1. 錄音（會傳送到第三方）

  • 什麼時候錄：只有在使用者按住畫面上的錄音按鈕時才會啟動麥克風，放開手指即停止。App 不會在背景錄音，也不會自動開啟麥克風。
  • 錄多久：單次錄音長度上限為 10 秒（不足 0.5 秒者視為誤觸，直接捨棄，不會傳送）。
  • 存在哪裡：錄音以 WAV 格式（16 kHz、單聲道）寫入裝置的系統暫存目錄，不會存進相簿、檔案 App 或任何雲端空間。
  • 用途：僅用於將孩子的發音與圖卡上的詞語比對，判斷發音是否正確、給予回饋與計分。
  • 之後怎麼處理：評分流程結束後（無論成功、失敗或中途取消），該暫存音檔會立即被刪除。

2. 訂閱購買

  • 購買流程完全由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 處理，我們不會取得您的付款資料、姓名或 Apple ID／Google 帳號。
  • 本 App 只會取得購買狀態（哪個方案、是否有效、到期日），用來解鎖內容，並顯示在「我的訂閱」頁面。
  • 僅限 Android：為了顯示方案與到期日，本 App 會把 Google Play 的購買憑證（purchase token）傳送到我們的雲端函式（見第二節第 2 點），由它向 Google Play 查詢後回傳結果。購買憑證是 Google Play 產生的隨機字串，不含任何個人資料，也不會被保存。

4. 我們「不」蒐集的資料

  • 不蒐集姓名、生日、性別、住址、電話、電子郵件等個人身分資料。
  • 不蒐集位置資訊、通訊錄、相簿、行事曆等裝置內容。
  • 不蒐集廣告識別碼（IDFA／AAID），不做使用者追蹤、行為分析或個人化廣告。
  • 本 App 未內建任何廣告、分析（Analytics）或第三方追蹤 SDK。
  • 本 App 沒有社群功能、留言、聊天或使用者間的互動，孩子無法公開分享任何內容。

二、第三方服務

1. Microsoft Azure AI Speech（發音評分）

本 App 的發音評分由 Microsoft Azure AI Speech 的語音轉文字與發音評估（Pronunciation Assessment）功能提供，在 Azure 的日本東部區域處理。

  • 傳送的內容：僅有前述的短音檔、目前圖卡上的詞語（例如「蘋果」，作為比對用的標準答案），以及該詞語的語言（zh-TW 或 en-US）。
  • 不會傳送：裝置識別碼、帳號資訊、姓名或任何可辨識個人身分的欄位。音檔不會附帶任何身分標記。
  • 回傳的內容：辨識出的文字與發音分數，僅在裝置端即時用於顯示回饋，不會被存下來。
  • Microsoft 端的處理：依 Microsoft 的官方文件，即時語音辨識與發音評估的音訊只在伺服器記憶體中處理，不會被保留或儲存。詳細說明請見 Speech to text 的資料、隱私與安全性說明 與 Microsoft 隱私權聲明。

2. Google Firebase（內容下載與訂閱查詢）

  • Cloud Storage：本 App 會下載圖卡圖片與音檔、主題清單，以及一個記錄最新 App 版本的小檔案。這些都只是下載，不會上傳任何關於您或孩子的資料。
  • Cloud Functions（僅限 Android）：第一節第 2 點所說的訂閱查詢，在 Google Cloud 的台灣區域執行。它只會收到購買憑證，用來向 Google Play 查詢，不會保存。iOS 版不使用此功能。
  • 詳細說明請見 Firebase 隱私權說明。

3. Apple App Store 與 Google Play

  • 付款與訂閱由 Apple 或 Google 依其各自的隱私權政策處理（見第一節第 2 點）。
  • 「幫我們評分」使用系統內建的評分視窗，本 App 看不到您填寫的內容。

與所有網路連線一樣，上述服務會收到裝置的 IP 位址以便回應，並可能為了安全與維運目的，短期保留標準的技術紀錄。我們不會用這些資訊辨識、追蹤或分析任何人。除上述服務之外，本 App 不會將任何資料傳送給其他第三方，也不會為了行銷或任何目的出售、出租或交換使用者資料。

購買與設定頁面前都設有家長驗證（一道算術題），孩子無法自行進入。

Introduction

KidSpeech (the “App”) is a speech-learning application designed for children aged 2–3, developed and operated by CityFun (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the App processes, why, and where it goes.

Summary: The App requires no sign-up and no account. It does not collect names, birthdates, phone numbers, email addresses, or any personally identifying information.

When the child holds the record button, a short audio clip and the word being practiced are sent to Microsoft Azure AI Speech to score the pronunciation. The clip is deleted from the device immediately afterwards, and Microsoft does not store it. We never retain, analyze, or sell recordings.

The App also downloads flashcard content from Google Firebase, and subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. None of these send us information that identifies you or your child.

1. Data We Process

1.1 Voice recordings (sent to a third party)

  • When: The microphone activates only while the user holds down the record button on screen, and stops the moment it is released. The App never records in the background and never activates the microphone on its own.
  • Length: A single recording is capped at 10 seconds. Recordings shorter than 0.5 seconds are treated as accidental taps and discarded without being sent.
  • Where it is stored: The recording is written as a WAV file (16 kHz, mono) to the device’s system temporary directory. It is never saved to the photo library, the Files app, or any cloud storage.
  • Purpose: Solely to assess how the word was pronounced compared with the word on the card, in order to provide feedback and a score.
  • Afterwards: Once the scoring step finishes — whether it succeeds, fails, or is cancelled — the temporary audio file is deleted immediately.

1.2 Subscription purchases

  • Purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details, your name, or your Apple ID / Google account.
  • The App only receives the purchase status (which plan, whether it is active, and when it expires), which it uses to unlock content and to show it on the “My Subscription” page.
  • Android only: To show the plan and expiry date, the App sends the Google Play purchase token to our cloud function (see 2.2), which looks it up with Google Play and returns the result. The token is a random string issued by Google Play that contains no personal information, and it is not stored.

1.4 What we do NOT collect

  • No names, birthdates, gender, addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or calendar access.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID); no user tracking, behavioral profiling, or personalized advertising.
  • No advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs are embedded in the App.
  • No social features, comments, chat, or user-to-user interaction. Children cannot publicly share any content.

2. Third-Party Services

2.1 Microsoft Azure AI Speech (pronunciation scoring)

Pronunciation scoring is provided by the speech to text and Pronunciation Assessment features of Microsoft Azure AI Speech, processed in Azure’s Japan East region.

  • What is sent: Only the short audio clip described above, the word on the current card (for example “apple”) to compare against, and its language (zh-TW or en-US).
  • What is not sent: No device identifiers, account information, names, or any personally identifying fields. The audio carries no identity marker.
  • What comes back: The recognized text and pronunciation scores, used on-device in real time to show feedback and never stored.
  • Handling by Microsoft: According to Microsoft’s documentation, audio sent for real-time speech recognition and pronunciation assessment is processed only in server memory and is not retained or stored. See Data, privacy, and security for Speech to text and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

2.2 Google Firebase (content downloads and subscription lookup)

  • Cloud Storage: The App downloads flashcard images and audio, the topic list, and a small file with the latest app version. These are downloads only; nothing about you or your child is uploaded.
  • Cloud Functions (Android only): The subscription lookup described in 1.2 runs on Google Cloud in the Taiwan region. It only receives the purchase token, uses it to ask Google Play, and does not store it. The iOS version does not use this.
  • See the Firebase privacy information.

2.3 Apple App Store and Google Play

  • Payments and subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies (see 1.2).
  • The “Rate This App” prompt uses the system’s own rating dialog; the App does not see what you enter.

As with any internet request, these services receive the device’s IP address in order to respond, and may keep standard technical logs for a limited time for security and operations. We do not use this to identify, track, or profile anyone. Apart from the services above, the App transmits no data to any third party. We never sell, rent, or trade user data for marketing or any other purpose.

3. Children’s Privacy

The App’s primary users are young children, so it is deliberately built for minimal collection: no accounts, no identification, no advertising, no behavioral tracking. Recordings exist only long enough to score a single pronunciation attempt and are then deleted.

Purchases and Settings are placed behind a parental gate (a math question), so children cannot reach them on their own.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we may have inadvertently obtained your child’s personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will investigate and delete it promptly.

We recommend that a parent or guardian supervise young children while using the App and manage microphone permissions in the device’s system settings.

5. Retention and Security

  • We have no user accounts and maintain no database of user data.
  • Recordings exist only briefly as temporary files on the device and are deleted after scoring. Microsoft does not store them, and they are never retained anywhere we can access.
  • Purchase tokens sent to our cloud function (Android only) are used for a single lookup and are not stored.
  • All network connections use encrypted HTTPS.

6. Your Rights

Because we retain no data that identifies you or your child, there is no personal data record for us to look up, correct, or export. You control on-device data as follows:

  • Deletion: Uninstall the App or clear its data to remove all local data.
  • Stop transmission: Revoke the microphone permission and no audio will be sent; disable network access to stop all transmission.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region that grants you data subject rights, you are welcome to contact us at the address below.
